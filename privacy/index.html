Privacy

What this site collects, and why.

Openhours · Christchurch, New Zealand · statement dated 12 September 2026

What is collected

The contact form collects your name, your email address, the firm you enter (optional), and your message.

The Set-Aside Calculator, when it's live, runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you type are not sent anywhere unless you choose email me the breakdown — then your name, your email address, and the answers you entered are sent so the breakdown can be emailed to you.

Nothing else. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

Why, and where it goes

Contact form details are used to reply to you, and for nothing else. Calculator details are used to send you the breakdown, and for nothing else. Both are delivered by email through an automation service to Jayden Ashby's mailbox. They are not sold, shared, or added to a marketing list.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record the IP address of a request in its own server logs; Openhours has no access to those and does not use them.

How long it's kept

As long as it's needed to deal with your message or request, and no longer. Ask and it's deleted.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information Openhours holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Use the contact form. If you're not satisfied with the response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

When the tool is on someone else's site

A practice that installs the Set-Aside Calculator on its own website receives the leads from it directly. That practice's privacy statement covers those details, not this one.
