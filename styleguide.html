Openhours — style guide · not linked, not indexed

Paper by day. Ink by night. One lamp left on.

Every colour, size and space on the site is decided here, once, and named. Pages use the names. Change it here and every page follows.

1 · Colour

Three colours. The accent in three places.

Lamp is used for links, the one call-to-action button, and the calculator's headline number. Nowhere else. Contrast ratios below were checked with the WCAG formula, not guessed.

Paper#FAF9F6 · day background
Ink#0F1319 · night background · 17.7:1 on Paper
Lamp#E9A23B · surfaces; text on Ink 8.6:1
Lamp, as text on Paper#9A5F10 · 4.96:1
Muted, day#5A5F6B · 6.1:1
Muted, night#A8ACB5 · 8.2:1
