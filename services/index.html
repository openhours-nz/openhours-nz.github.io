Services & pricing

Three things. Not a menu.

Openhours does three things for accounting practices, in this order. Everything starts with the first one.

1

The tool, on your site

The Set-Aside Calculator, in your colours, under your name, on the website you already have.

  • One line to install — WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, anything. No rebuild.
  • Every "email me the breakdown" lands in your inbox with all six answers attached.
  • Starts with a free 14-day preview at a private link, personalised to your firm. Show your team, decide.
  • Install is the moment you pay. If you don't install, the preview comes down and you owe nothing.

Priced once, on the preview. No surprises after.

2

A website built to hold tools

Like this one. Fast, plain, no page-builder — made so tools can live in it.

  • Your existing content moved across; nothing lost.
  • Loads in about a second on a phone. Titles, descriptions and link previews done properly.
  • You own every file. No platform, no monthly licence, no lock-in.
  • Fixed quote after a 30-minute call. Half up front, half on launch.

Usually the second conversation, not the first.

3

Automation, after the first yes

Once the tool has proven a lead exists, the follow-up can run itself.

  • Instant reply to every lead with a booking link, in your voice.
  • An after-hours assistant on your site that answers the common questions and books the call — never gives advice.
  • Document chasing at year-end, so you're not.

Scoped and priced per job, with a deposit. Only offered once there's something to automate.

Straight answers

Things people ask before they ask.

Do I have to move my website?
No. The tool goes onto the site you have. If you want a new site later, that's a separate conversation.
Who owns it?
You do. The tool on your site is yours; the leads are yours; there's no platform to be locked into.
What if it doesn't bring leads?
That's what the free preview is for — you see it working before you pay. And if I don't think it suits your firm, I'll say so before you spend anything.
Is the calculator advice?
No, and it says so on the page. It's an estimate that starts a conversation with you. Structure and tax advice stay where they belong — with the accountant.
Why is it so cheap?
Because it's one tool, done properly, installed many times — not a bespoke build every time. The bespoke work is what comes after.
